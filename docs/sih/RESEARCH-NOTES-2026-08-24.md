# SIH 2026 Planning Research Notes

## Official SIH project-implementation guidance

Source: <https://sih.gov.in/projectImplementation> (retrieved 2026-08-24).

The official page states that hackathon projects are feasibility checks and require further work before reliable implementation or field deployment. It gives a typical post-winning development/implementation horizon of six months to one year. It requests a detailed project plan covering implementation, required software/hardware, expert support, and timelines. It calls for coordination by a ministry/department or an appropriate technical agency/expert panel, at least one experienced technical mentor, regular monitoring, institutional consent, cybersecurity expertise for software solutions, and quarterly status reports. It also says that winning-team IP resides with students while the concerned ministry has lifetime free access, subject to the page's stated conditions; teams must use verified open-source components and acknowledge them appropriately.

## Supplied SIH 2026 Guidelines PDF

Source: `/home/ubuntu/upload/SIH2026Guidelines.pdf`.

Key planning requirements extracted from the supplied document include:

| Requirement area | Extracted requirement |
|---|---|
| Eligibility and nomination | Students are entered through their college SPOC and internal-hackathon selection; the PDF states a maximum of 50 teams per institute and 100 per university. |
| Team | Six student members from the same college, including a leader; at least one female team member is mandatory. |
| Deadline | SPOC registration starts in July 2026. The supplied PDF sets 20 September 2026 as the deadline for team nomination and idea submission, and states that a problem statement may close once 500 ideas have been submitted. |
| Submission fields | Team leader verifies team/authorization details, chooses a problem statement, enters title and description, and uploads an idea presentation PDF. |
| Selection | Novelty, complexity, clarity/detail, feasibility, practicability, sustainability, scale of impact, UX, and future-work potential. |
| Mentors | Up to two industry or academic mentors are optional after shortlisting; the supplied PDF describes five years of relevant experience as the eligibility expectation. |
| Finale | The supplied PDF proposes an offline December 2026 grand finale, with photo ID and consent-letter requirements for selected teams. |
| IP and originality | The supplied PDF says IP for winning ideas is split with the problem-statement industry or set by mutual agreement, and proposals must be new and not previously presented in another event/program. |

## Official SIH portal context

Source: <https://www.sih.gov.in/> (retrieved 2026-08-24).

The portal describes SIH as a nationwide student initiative for real-world problem solving. Its process flow places SPOC registration and an internal campus hackathon before team recommendation, idea submission, evaluation, and result publication.
