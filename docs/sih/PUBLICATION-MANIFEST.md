## Explicit exclusions

The repository does not publish the supplied SIH guideline PDF, supplied presentation PPTX, uploaded planning/reference files, any portal screenshots or confirmations, team member data, institutional authorization letters, mentor consent, personal signatures, real identity/asset records, credentials, keys, secret material, or any real deployment evidence. These records belong to the appropriate authorized party and must be handled only through the approved portal or institutional process.

## no-mistakes process boundary

The upstream [no-mistakes repository](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes) was inspected at `c878873c5573a7cff6ed8b780eb0e1c53a8d8fc7`; its `LICENSE` is MIT. Its disposable-worktree, validate-before-push concept informed this manifest and the existing protected-PR evidence flow. The upstream proxy, command, hooks, agents, auto-fix behavior, configuration, source, and dependencies were **not** installed, executed, copied, or trusted. No-mistakes is not an independent audit, SIH validator, or merge authority.

## Required pre-push checks

Before publishing a future SIH artifact, confirm its project authorship, redaction, citations, license/provenance, claims boundary, and documentation-table validity. Then run the repository’s relevant checks and use the protected review workflow. A non-author approval remains mandatory for protected merge.
